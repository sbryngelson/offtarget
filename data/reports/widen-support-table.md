## Reproduction gate

Each failing legal cell (a counted rejection, a wrong answer, a crash, a host fallback, a failed probe) carries the reproduction gate's status (offload.engine.gate, results/gate/verdicts.jsonl, and the decisions of catalog/gate_queue.yaml): **confirmed** (its own rerun verdict, or one inherited from a compile-time defect signature), **decided** (a decision; it counts when the decision says counts_as: failure), **unchecked** (the gate did not rerun it), **nondeterministic**, **ours** (a recorded ruling says the failure is our build's), the other verdicts, or **ungated** (no verdict yet).
