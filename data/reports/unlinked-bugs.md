# Confirmed catalog bugs without a finding

Generated by `python3 -I tools/confirm.py backlog --write` from results/confirm/batch_full.jsonl and catalog/unlinked_bugs.yaml (offload/engine/confirm/backlog.py). A cell is listed when the confirmer calls it a bug (compiler_bug, unified_memory_failure, copy_mode_failure, arch_specific) and its dedupe found no finding, duplicate or related. tests/confirm/test_confirm_backlog.py fails when a group appears without a recorded reason or a group's size changes.

## How the backlog was closed

A review of the catalog on 2026-10-23 found 1,016 catalog cells with a bug verdict and no finding, draft or related link (974 with neither a duplicate nor a related link). After the findings index gained its data-derived gfx90a scope (134 cells became instances), 840 cells in 50 signature groups remained. Each group's representative case went through `tools/confirm.py batch --draft` (offline, stored evidence; results/confirm/drafts/); every draft kept the verdict compiler_bug with the catalog program itself as the reproducer (catalog programs are one-feature programs of 30-60 lines; no minimization step was needed to keep the verdict). Four groups were explained by an existing finding whose README text names the programs, and were linked by a `programs` pattern; 33 groups (37 signature groups: the four AFAR linear ICE groups share one stack, checked by compiling them, and the two AFAR workdistribute groups another) were promoted to data/findings/catalog/ (`tools/confirm.py promote`, then the index entry widened to the group's compilers and programs). The C nohost groups left the backlog because the maintainer withdrew the gfortran/gcc witnesses (R12a cannot fire). What remains is listed below with the reason it has no finding.
