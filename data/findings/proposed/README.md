# data/findings/proposed

Findings proposed from outside the project, as `tools/new_finding.py --proposal` writes them: the program with its
header, `NAME.rows.jsonl` (the recorded results it was drafted from) and `controls/`. Nothing here is published or
indexed. A maintainer reruns each one in the four gate environments and on the other compilers, adds its site rows
and citations, checks its legality, and moves it to its folder under `data/findings/` with its index entry
([CONTRIBUTING.md](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md), "Add or correct a finding").
