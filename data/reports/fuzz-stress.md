# A smarter fuzzer and stress testing with very complex programs

Status: provisional. Nothing is reported externally. The wrong-answer candidates were audited and re-reviewed: promoted five findings and eight more to data/findings/stress/
(README section "Stress programs"). The remaining open items are under "Limits and what is not done".

## Summary

* **Usage profile** (`catalog/fuzz_profile.json`, `offload/engine/usage.py`, `offload/engine/fuzz_profile.py`). It
  measures the application corpus (six manifest codes; MFC from its generated sources), the other
  offload codes under corpus/apps and the data/bench/wild dev half: 1,550 offload regions in total. Real
  maxima:
  - lists and clauses: private list 159 (MFC), map list 31 (CloudSC), clauses per directive 13 (RSBench),
    directive continuation lines 17 (CloudSC);
  - routines and modules: device-routine arguments 78 and locals 254 (CloudSC), device-resident module
    variables 113 (MFC), USE ONLY list 19;
  - region shape: scalars referenced by one region 20, loop nest depth 7, collapse 5;
  - derived types and calls: type nesting depth 3 and no OO features in offload code; module DAG depth 17,
    device call chain 8 deep crossing files 3 times;
  - size: 430 offload regions in one program (MFC).

  Every stress sweep covers the real maximum and goes 2x, 4x and 10x beyond it.
* **Steering** (`offload/engine/fuzz_steer.py`). Profile-weighted sampling-importance-resampling over fuzz-4s50
  candidates, with a boost for feature pairs enriched in failing real kernels and per-family feedback
  (neighbourhoods of new failures up, saturated ones down). Templates from failing real kernels are
  generated by the stress generator.
* **Stress generator.**
  - Programs: Fortran OpenMP and OpenACC in `offload/engine/stress.py`, C OpenMP in `offload/engine/stress_c.py`.
    There are 31 axes, and every one has a size knob and a size sweep.
  - Oracle: one Python model per kernel, and each kernel prints its own value. The sequential elision
    (gfortran or gcc with no OpenMP) agrees with the model on every program it applies to: 0 mismatches
    among 903 programs. Cross-compiler agreement is a third source.
  - Legality gate: gfortran -std=f2018, AFAR flang or clang -fsyntax-only, and offload.engine.rules.
  - Labels: legal; illegal_probe (Fortran line 132, 255 continuation lines, 63-character names; implicit
    device procedures across compilation units, which for Fortran means across modules even in one file);
    unspecified (a polymorphic map in an OpenMP 6.0 program); impl_defined (C11 translation limits).
  - Shrinker: delta debugging in `offload/engine/stress_min.py`.
  - Executor: `offload/engine/stress_exec.py`. Limits are set inside the job: `ulimit -v` and `timeout
    --foreground --kill-after=10` on every compile, link and run, so they stay in the run's process group,
    which is killed (F5). Each row records compile time, peak RSS per step and the build
    directory. `STRESS_TMP` must be node-local.
* **Runs.**
  - AMD MI300A (Tuolumne): AFAR and CCE 19/20/21, XNACK 1 and 0, OpenMP Fortran, OpenACC Fortran (CCE) and
    OpenMP C.
  - NVIDIA A100 (wingtip): NVHPC 25.11 and 26.9 in separate memory, plus managed memory for the small
    sizes.
  - Layouts × build variants: O0, O2, O3, LTO/whole-program, no-inline, static library, NVHPC nordc.
  - AMD MI250X (Tioga, XNACK 0): a bounded run of the audit reproducers and the break-point probes (fix
).
  - Totals: 903 programs (442 OpenMP Fortran, 355 OpenACC Fortran, 106 C), 11,928 rows, the largest
    program 97,682 lines.
* **Headline results** (after's audit):
  - **Signatures.** 56 distinct failure signatures on legal, gated, model-confirmed programs (58 before
    the follow-up removed the two signatures of the split-CIF-record abort, rerun at clean paths).
  - **Promoted** to data/findings/stress/ after the confirmer said `compiler_bug`:
    - AFAR: a scalar with the TARGET attribute is treated as firstprivate;
    - CCE 19/20: a teams reduction wraps at 32 bits with 16,384 or more teams;
    - CCE 21: an explicit map of a declare target scalar loses the device write;
    - CCE: lastprivate is wrong with `num_threads` on `target teams distribute parallel do simd
      collapse(2)`;
    - craycc `-flto` drops the offload code (written by hand).
  - **Audited, new, not promoted** (confirmer `needs_human`, controls run):
    - AFAR C misses the implicit lastprivate map;
    - NVHPC: the TARGET scalar, the declare target scalar, the OpenACC derived-type component, the
      implicit lastprivate map (Fortran) and lastprivate even with a map (C);
    - CCE: the OpenACC `declare create` scalar in `serial`.
  - **The "path-dependent" crash is pinned and promoted.** CCE ftn (17.0.1 to 21.0.4) copies
    its compiler-message records in 1023-character chunks and stops at a chunk that starts with `-1`; an
    optimizer message that names the source path splits there when the path puts `-1` at offset 1023,
    and the compile aborts (`free(): invalid pointer`) or silently drops the later messages
    (`stress/cce-ftn-cif-record-split-abort.f90`).
  - **Capacity.**
    - CCE's runtime limit "Large args not supported": 310 mapped arrays (10x the real maximum), 256

    - CCE compile time: over 900 s for MFC's 430 kernels (AFAR 32 s, NVHPC 37 s).
    - MI250X shows the same break points.
* **Equal budget.** Recounted by confirmer signature, with the sibling rule. The arms used unequal CPU
  (stress 5.1 task-CPU-hours against 11.6 and 9.2), so they are **not ranked**. Uniform and steered find

## 1. Real-code guidance

**Profile** (`python3 -I tools/campaigns/stress.py profile`, committed as catalog/fuzz_profile.json). The scanner
handles the following:

* Fortran free form: continuations (with `#` line markers and comments between them), directives and their
  clauses with list lengths, offload regions (an OpenMP `target` compute construct, an OpenACC
  parallel/kernels/serial construct; combined loop constructs end with their loop).
* Per region: features in one vocabulary (`dir:`, `clause:`, `map:`, `red:`, `base:`), loop depth,
  collapse, and scalars referenced without a clause (locals and module scalars).
* Procedures: arguments, locals, OPTIONAL/VALUE/CLASS dummies, device routines.
* Modules: variables and device-resident variables, USE ONLY lengths.
* Derived types: nesting, allocatable and pointer components, EXTENDS, type-bound procedures.
* C: pragmas with backslash continuations, structs, parameters, declare target blocks.
* A small preprocessor for #if/#ifdef with the build's defines. MFC's fypp output keeps both GPU branches
  under `#if defined(MFC_OpenACC)`.

Hand counts are checked in tests/test_offload.py.

| code | regions | private max | map max | dev. routine args / locals | module device vars | type depth | module DAG | device call depth (file crossings) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MFC (OpenMP and OpenACC builds) | 860 (430 per program) | 159 | 24 | 24 / 40 | 113 | 3 | 17 | 8 (3) |
| CloudSC (corpus/apps) | 12 | 22 | 31 | 78 / 254 | 38 | 1 | 2 | 1 (1) |
| HPC training examples | 466 | 4 | 5 | 0 / 0 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 1 (1) |
| PRK, BabelStream, miniWeather, XSBench, RSBench, NWChem, POT3D | 1 to 56 each | ≤ 7 | ≤ 22 | 0 | 0 | ≤ 1 | ≤ 3 | ≤ 1 |
| data/bench/wild dev | 50 | 2 | 3 | 2 / 1 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 1 (1) |

No offload code in the corpus uses CLASS dummies, type-bound procedures, SELECT TYPE or EXTENDS in or near
offload code. The `oo` axis therefore exercises features real codes do not use yet, and is measured as
portability data.

**Weights and boost** (`offload/engine/fuzz_steer.py`). The generator code is unchanged: fuzz-4s50 programs
replay by seed. A steered seed draws 12 candidates (seeds `3,000,000 + 12*seed + j`) and keeps one with
probability proportional to its weight:

* the per-region independent-feature density ratio, real against fuzz-4s50 frequencies (400 programs),
  tempered by alpha = 0.5 and clipped to [1/20, 20];
* ×1.5 per boost pair: feature pairs enriched in failing real regions, meaning MFC's two kernels named by
  CCE's device-link crashes and the 17 failing dev cases; mostly defaultmap combinations;
* the feedback multiplier: ×2 near (Jaccard ≥ 0.5) a program with a new signature, ×0.3 near a signature
  seen 5 or more times, per compiler family. A campaign that runs every family uses the mean.

**Templates** (`stress.TemplateKernel`), the structures of failing real kernels, varied by size:

* `team_private`: team-private array, also PRK transpose tile32;
* `declared_alloc`: MFC under CCE at unified memory;
* `mfc_cbc`: m_cbc.fpp:597, with long private lists of scalars and arrays, bounds from components of a
  device module derived type, sequential inner loops, device routines with array arguments, and a module
  logical branch;
* `mfc_bubbles`: m_bubbles_EE.fpp:186, with an array of derived type with a pointer 3-D component through
  index arrays, collapse(3), private arrays, a device function with an OPTIONAL keyword argument, and an
  atomic counter.

## 2. Stress mode

**Axes** (`stress.AXES`; `<axis>.<variant>` splits an axis so one rejected variant does not hide the
others; default sweeps in `stress.SWEEPS`, profile maxima added):

| group | axes |
|---|---|
| size of the program | `kernels` (to 4,300 kernels, 97,682 lines), `random` (every axis together), `tu` / `tuimp` (module DAG and device call chains up to 32 deep across files; submodules; bind(C) to C) |
| nesting | `nest` (up to 70 loops, collapse at full depth, split teams/parallel/simd), `calls` (device chains up to 80, recursion) |
| long lists | `private`, `firstprivate`, `lastprivate`, `shared`, `map`, `reduction`, `udr` (user-defined mixed with intrinsic), `depend`, `declare` (module variables), `sections` (many sections of one present array, strided updates), `clauses` (every allowed clause of `target teams distribute parallel do simd`, repeated to 256 clauses), `contlines` (up to 300 continuation lines), `names` (31 to 128 characters) |
| long variable lists | `locals` (to 2,540), `args` (to 780 dummies: VALUE, by reference, arrays, derived types, OPTIONAL by keyword), `useonly` (to 1,024), `capture` (to 1,024 implicitly firstprivate scalars) |
| types | `dtnest` (nesting to 30 with allocatable and pointer components, arrays of types, enter-data or one-region deep copy), `oo` (type-bound pass/nopass, generic and operator bindings, EXTENDS chains, abstract types, CLASS + SELECT TYPE, PDT, polymorphic map) |
| scalars | `scalars` (17 OpenMP and 14 OpenACC routes, below) |
| synchronization, compile time, runtime | `mixed` (reductions + atomics + named criticals), `exprs` (2,048-term expressions, SELECT CASE of 2,048 cases, 2,048 specializations), `async` (many nowait/depend target tasks, enter/exit chains; OpenACC async queues), `runtime` (2^20 to 2^33 iterations, 65,536 teams, allocations up to 64 GiB, declared in the header) |
| real shapes | `template` (above) |

The C generator (`stress_c.C_AXES`) covers:

* lists, depend and is_device_ptr;
* backslash-continued pragmas;
* identifiers to 4,096 characters and functions with 780 parameters (impl_defined past C11's minimum
  limits);
* nested structs with pointer members, deep-copied by a map list or `declare mapper`;
* VLAs and scalar routes;
* device call chains over files, with a header of `declare target` (5.0) or `begin declare target` (5.1)
  prototypes.

**Legality.** Each program states its language level (F2018 free form, C11) and OpenMP 5.2 in 5.0
spellings (so NVHPC reads them) or OpenACC 3.4. `stress.check_limits` measures the rendered text, and a
program past a limit is an illegal probe: line 132 (F2018 6.3.2.1), 255 continuation lines (6.3.2.4), 63
characters (6.2.2). F2023 limits were not tested: gfortran 13 has no `-std=f2023`.

* Implicit declare target across compilation units is an illegal probe.
  - OpenMP 5.2 §13.8 p.284 makes a procedure referenced in a target construct an implicit enter list
    item, and §5.8.4 p.158 creates its device version only in the definition's compilation unit.
  - For Fortran a compilation unit is a program unit (§1.2.2 p.3; OpenACC 3.4 §2.15.1 p.91, "program unit
    being compiled"). A chain through other modules is therefore cross-unit even in one file, so `tuimp`
    is a probe in every layout (F7). The C `tuimp` chains cross translation units.
* Mapping a polymorphic variable is unspecified in OpenMP 6.0 (§7.9.6 p.287), so the `oo.polymap` programs
  declare 6.0. OpenMP 5.2 only restricts polymorphic allocatables in firstprivate, lastprivate and
  copyprivate (F9).
* Type-bound procedures in target regions are legal with declare target on every procedure (5.0/5.2 §1.2.6
  mappable type).
* PDTs are legal (5.2 §5.8.3: the corresponding item has the original's type parameters).
* The F2008 features 5.0 §1.7 lists as unspecified (submodules, VALUE) are legal because the programs are
  5.2 programs.

Generator defects:

* found by the gate and fixed: the deep-nest line length, and the split-chain reduction map;
* found by the review and fixed: `lin` shared in every `clauses` and `random` program, a data race (F1;
  the linter's race rule is now a test);
* not a fix, a gate rejection: flang rejects `in_reduction` on the combined construct, so those `clauses`
  programs fail the gate and are not counted.

Each program and row records its axis revision (`stress.AXIS_REV`), and records of an older revision are
dropped (`stress_report.current`).

**Default scalar residency** (`ScalarsKernel`, one kernel per route; cites in `stress.ROUTE_CITES`). A
single device thread reads each scalar and writes `2*s+1`. Only defined observations enter the value: the
device read of the host value, and the host value afterwards (unchanged for private copies, the write for
tofrom). Undefined ones print `OBS` lines; 5.2 §1.4.2 p.27 makes a write to a variable with a non-shared
corresponding variable leave the other undefined. Each row below is one route and what it tests:

* `implicit`, `firstprivate`, `private`, `dm_firstprivate`, `byref`, `value`: private copies.
* `to`, `dm_to`: the host value afterwards is undefined.
* `from`, `tofrom`, `dm_tofrom`, `reduction`: mapped.
* `target_attr` (5.2 §5.8.1 p.148: a TARGET scalar is tofrom).
* `present_implicit`: enter data, then a host write, then an implicit reference. Firstprivate takes the
  host value.
* `present_update`: update to, map tofrom, update from.
* `declare`: declare target with updates.
* `component`: a derived type is not a scalar variable, so tofrom.
* OpenACC (3.4 §2.6.2 p.41): implicit in parallel/serial (firstprivate) and kernels (copy), visible data
  clause of an enclosing data construct, enter data then implicit, declare create, derived-type component
  (aggregate: copy).

| route | AFAR | CCE 19/20/21 | NVHPC 25.11/26.9 |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenMP `target_attr` | **wrong** (firstprivate) | correct | **wrong** (firstprivate) |
| OpenMP `declare` (declare target scalar, update from) | correct | correct | **wrong**: the device write is lost |
| OpenMP `dm_firstprivate` | rejected (not implemented) | correct | runs on the host under mandatory (host_fallback) |
| OpenMP `reduction` with 200 scalars | correct | run error (Large args) | correct |
| OpenACC `declare` (declare create module scalar in serial) | n/a | **wrong** (treated as firstprivate) | correct |
| OpenACC `component` (derived-type scalar component, implicit) | n/a | correct | **wrong** (not copied back) |
| OpenACC `reduction` with 200 scalars | n/a | **wrong** (CCE 20/21) | correct |
| every other route (OpenMP 17, OpenACC 14) | correct | correct | correct |

The same results hold in both memory modes: XNACK 1 and 0, and separate and managed. The only
memory-mode-dependent observations are the `OBS` values of `to` and `copyin` (undefined by the
specification).

**Oracle and limits.** The Python model; the elision as its check; cross-compiler agreement in the report.
Compile limit: 900 s for each step and the whole build, 32 GiB virtual per compiler process (16 GiB on
wingtip). Run limit: 120 s (600 s for runtime). All are enforced inside the job and recorded in each row.
A timeout or out-of-memory counts only if a smaller sibling compiled within the limits.

## What the stress programs found

There are 56 distinct signatures (`offload.engine.stress_report.findings`) on legal programs that passed the
gate and whose model the elision confirmed. Rows of an older axis revision are dropped.

Fix ran the brief's audit on every wrong-answer candidate. Each candidate got a hand reproducer
and a control program that differs only in the construct under test; all sources are in
`data/reports/fuzz-stress-audit/` (`tools/campaigns/stress_audit.py`, runs in `results/stress/audit/`). Every
reproducer was run as follows:

* on MI300A, at XNACK 1 and 0, on AFAR and CCE 19/20/21;
* on MI250X (Tioga, XNACK 0), on all four;
* on A100 (wingtip), on NVHPC 25.11 and 26.9 in separate and managed memory;
* through the confirmer (`tools/confirm.py case... --run --draft`).

Confirmed (`compiler_bug`, rule R20) candidates were promoted to `data/findings/stress/` (README section
"Stress programs"). The confirmer gave `needs_human` (R22, "expected provisional: only model") when no
second source of the expected value exists on that machine. It does not draw expected values across
machines, and the elision did not apply to these programs. Fix promoted those the
a second check accepted: a same-compiler control gives the expected value, and another family gives it on the
same program (a result on another machine counts for these deterministic single-construct programs) or
no family can, for a stated reason.

Notes:

* **Candidate 6, restated.** The trigger is a teams reduction with 16,384 or more teams, not the
  iteration count. The `runtime.iters` shape (no `num_teams`) is correct on CCE 19/20 up to 2^30, and on
  CCE 21 at 2^32. The `runtime.alloc` shape (fill, then reduce, an allocatable of n elements) is wrong on
  CCE 19/20 from 2^22 elements (2^20 correct; MI300A and MI250X). On CCE 21 it is wrong at 2^32 elements
  (`2147378998` instead of `29949`, reproduced in a second run, f4-wrap) while the same 2^32 iterations
  without the array are correct. That alloc case was reduced and promoted:
  the fill loop over 2^32 iterations skips its last 256-iteration block
  (data/findings/cce21-alloc-2e32-sum-wrong.f90).
* **Candidate 7, restated.** The earlier evidence came from a racy program: `lin` was shared in every
  `clauses` and `random` program (F1). With `lin` private, the 14-clause stress program is
  still wrong on CCE. Dropping each clause in turn (the variants that matter are committed as

## Equal budget: does the fuzzer get smarter?

Each arm ran on one MI300A node with 24 workers. The three started at the same time on identical nodes
(1011, 1012, 1013) with the same toolchains (AFAR and CCE 19/20/21) in copy mode and a 3,000 s budget.

Fix recounts the arms by the **confirmer's signature** (`stress_report.confirm_signature`; F6):
* a failure that duplicates an indexed finding counts as that finding (`findings.dedupe`);
* otherwise an ICE counts by its compiler-source site, an abort by its signal, and any other failure by
  its message key;
* an unlinked wrong answer counts once per compiler family, because the confirmer keeps a wrong answer by
  its verdict until it is minimized and linked;
* compile timeouts and out-of-memory count only with a smaller sibling that compiled;
* the stress arm's `clauses` and `random` rows (68, racy before F1) are dropped.

**The arms are not comparable as a bug rate, so they are not ranked.** The reasons:

* **Unequal CPU.** The equal wall-clock budget gave very unequal CPU: the stress arm's batches waited for
  their slowest compile, so it used 5.1 task-CPU-hours against 11.6. The stress arm also lost its last
  batch to the job limit.
* **Population-dependent counts.** The counts depend on which compiler-source sites the CCE ICEs land on;
  each CCE release reports its own site for one message, which gives several signatures per family.
* **Collapsed wrong answers.** Unlinked wrong answers are one signature per family whatever their
  number: 28 AFAR and 241 CCE wrong-answer rows in the uniform arm, 91 and 375 in the steered arm.

What the data does show:

* Per task CPU-hour, the uniform and steered arms are within noise (2.8 and 2.9).
* The steered arm produced more wrong-answer rows: 466 rows from 1,344 programs, against 269 rows from
  2,064 programs for uniform. Whether those are new bugs needs minimization.
* The stress arm's signatures include the compile-size ICE ("optcg terminated", SIGABRT) and the CCE
  "Large args" run error. These come from program size, which the fuzz arms do not reach.

The bugs promoted came from the stress **axis sweeps** and their audit, not from the
equal-budget arm.

## Limits and what is not done

* **Promoted later:** the CCE 21 alloc shape at 2^32,
  (data/findings/cce21-alloc-2e32-sum-wrong.f90: the fill loop skips its last 256 iterations). (The CCE
  ftn path-dependent abort was promoted by hand: the confirmer cannot choose a build path.)
* **The shrinker** reaches one kernel for wrong answers. Size-threshold failures (ICE at 512 kernels,
  Large args at 310 maps) do not shrink below the threshold by nature.
* **NVHPC failures were not shrunk on wingtip** (no gate there). The NVHPC audit used hand reproducers.
* **The stress arm's batch barrier** halves its CPU utilization.
* **F2023 limits** were not tested (gfortran 13). Fortran `linear` and `order(concurrent)` are not on the
  every-clause directive (conflicts; `stress.NOT_FILLED`). `aligned` and `has_device_addr` are excluded.
* **CCE's compile times** include the module prelude in the wall time. Only the per-step times
  (`/usr/bin/time`) are used for scaling. The first minutes of each sweep ran 24 compiles on a node, so
  small-program CCE times of about 30 s include contention.
